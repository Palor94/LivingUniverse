# Living Universe

Living Universe is a persistent fictional world of adult characters with stable identities, appearances, personalities, relationships, histories, and evolving memories. The creator participates through an in-world character and separately administers the world. Characters know only what their experiences and knowledge permit, not everything the administrator knows.

The project starts directly in this folder. The earlier Personal AI Character Studio ZIP is unavailable and is not a dependency.

## Persistent visual identity

Recognizable character generation is a central purpose, not an accessory to storytelling. The target is Pákozdi Péter himself as an established fictional visual identity across clothing, scenes, environments, angles, and eventually motion, rather than a succession of similar people.

Each character needs a categorized reference library, an explicitly approved appearance profile, protected canonical revisions, reference-aware generation, per-character output feedback, and a history of successful and unsuccessful attempts. Future capabilities include multiple characters without feature mixing, compatible identity training, and video review.

The user reports approximately 31 previous Pákozdi references and recurring failures involving eyes, facial structure, and distinctive body proportions, including his projecting abdomen. These reports identify review priorities; they do not establish that current files are those references or that their contents are approved.

No generator is assumed to preserve identity perfectly. Canon protection is a software guarantee we can implement; visual fidelity is an empirical model capability we must evaluate.

## Wider world

Support approximately 50 initial characters and expansion through data rather than code changes. Tibor is the administrator; Palor is his in-world presence. Preserve objective events separately from subjective memories, beliefs, secrets, and mistaken assumptions. Allow administrator corrections, timeline branches, and eventual event-driven daily life without expensive continuous simulation by default.

The user is learning software development through practical work. Prefer small working increments, accessible explanations, private ownership of creative data, reliable backups, and replaceable AI providers.
